CashSense Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

The short version: CashSense has no internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere, because it has no ability to send anything at all. Everything the app reads and everything it remembers stays on your phone.

Who this is from

CashSense is an independent Android app that shows your bank balance as rupee notes and coins, so that spending feels like spending cash. This policy explains exactly what the app touches.

What CashSense reads

With your explicit permission, CashSense uses Android's notification listener to read notifications so it can spot payments as they happen. Android does not let any app subscribe to a chosen few apps' notifications — a listener is offered all of them — so CashSense filters immediately and narrowly:

  • Notifications from anything other than a payment app, a bank app, or an app that carries bank alerts (your SMS and email apps) are discarded without being examined.
  • Of what remains, only messages that actually describe a completed payment — an amount sitting beside a word like "debited", "credited" or "paid to" — are used. Everything else is dropped.

The app also uses your camera, only while you are on the QR scanner screen, to read a UPI QR code. No image or video is recorded, saved, or transmitted; the camera frame is decoded and discarded.

What CashSense stores on your phone

Held in a private database inside the app's own storage, readable by no other app:

  • The amount, direction (money in or out), and time of each transaction.
  • The bank's reference number (UPI RRN/UTR) for a payment, when the message quotes one. This is what lets the app tell one payment announced four times apart from four separate payments.
  • The package name of the app that announced the payment.
  • The text of the notification a transaction was read from, so you can check what the app saw if a figure ever looks wrong.
  • Your starting balance and your settings.

What CashSense does not do

  • No data leaves your device. The app does not hold the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting anything.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no cloud sync, no backup to any server.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, for any purpose. There is nothing to sell and no channel to sell it through.
  • No connection to your bank. CashSense never asks for and never receives your banking credentials, card numbers, PINs, OTPs, or account access. It only reads notifications your bank has already sent to your phone.

Your control over the data

  • Revoke access at any time in Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications, by turning CashSense off. Detection stops immediately.
  • Delete everything with "Reset wallet" inside the app, or by uninstalling it. Uninstalling removes the database with the app; nothing survives elsewhere, because nothing was ever stored elsewhere.

Children

CashSense is intended for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it reads or stores, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed with it. Any change that would involve data leaving the device would be announced in the app before it took effect.
